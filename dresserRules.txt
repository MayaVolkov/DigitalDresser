DIGITALDRESSER RULES
All temperatures are FEELS-LIKE (day.feelsHigh / day.feelsLow).
"Hours out" = 7am-9pm. Evening = 8pm-midnight.
"Cloudy" = Cloudy or Mostly Cloudy (Partly Cloudy / Mostly Sunny don't count).


BASE OUTFIT
Check top to bottom. The first one that matches wins.

  A. High below 32
     top:    Long sleeve + sweatshirt
     outer:  Coat
     bottom: Jeans or sweatpants
     shoes:  Sneakers, boots, or clogs (no flip-flops)

  B. Cloudy or rain 50%+, high 32-50
     top:    Sweatset
     bottom: (none, sweatset covers it)
     shoes:  Birks or clogs (if rain 50%+: sneakers or boots)

  C. Low 30-50, high up to 55
     top:    T-shirt + zip-up
     bottom: Jeans
     shoes:  Sneakers or clogs

  D. Low 50 or below, high 56-71 (warms up)
     top:    Tank top or t-shirt
     bottom: Jeans or leggings
     shoes:  Sneakers
     tip:    "Grab a zip-up for the morning"

  E. Low above 50, high up to 71
     top:    Short-sleeve top + zip-up
     bottom: Jeans or leggings
     shoes:  Sneakers or flip-flops

  F. High 72-89
     top:    T-shirt
     bottom: Jeans
     shoes:  Sneakers or flip-flops

  G. High 90+
     top:    Oversized t-shirt + shorts, OR a dress
     shoes:  Flip-flops
     if cloudy: shorts OR jeans

   H. Low below 30, high 32-55
     top:    Long sleeve + jacket, OR t-shirt + hoodie
     bottom: Jeans, sweatpants, or leggings
     shoes:  Sneakers, boots, or clogs (no flip-flops)



ADJUSTMENTS (apply after the base outfit)

  Rain 50%+ during hours out:
     add umbrella (no rain jacket)
     high below 60 -> shoes = Sneakers or boots (no clogs, Birks, or flip-flops)
     high 60+      -> flip-flops become sneakers

  Afternoon rain heads-up (3+ hours in a row over 50%):
     tip: "Heads up, it might rain starting at {start} until {end},
           if you're going to be out then grab an umbrella"

  Snowing:
     high 25 and up -> shoes = Sneakers or boots
     high below 25  -> shoes = Boots, outer = Coat

  Sunny: add sunglasses


JACKET COUNT
  Reasons for a jacket: windy (20+ mph), rain 50%+, cold (high below 50)
     rain alone          -> umbrella only, no jacket
     cold alone          -> no change (the base outfit already handles it)
     windy alone         -> Jacket
     any 2 or more       -> Coat


TIME RULES

  Workout (9-11am), using the 9am feels-like:
     below 50 -> add sweatpants + sweatshirt for workout
     50-70    -> add hoodie for workout

  Going out (Thursday, Friday, Saturday):
     if no evening hour feels above 50   -> add jacket to going-out outfit
     (on these days the widget switches to tomorrow at 9pm instead of 7pm)

  All tips show, not just one.
